Privacy Policy

ahmed clipper · Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ahmed clipper handles information when you use the local application on your computer.

1. Local-first processing

Videos, audio extracts, transcripts, clip files, and job history are stored on your PC under the app’s data folders. The developer does not receive a copy of your media through normal local use.

2. Settings and API keys

Optional settings such as AI provider API keys and TikTok client credentials are saved on your device so the app can call those services on your behalf. Keep those secrets private. Do not commit them to public repositories.

3. TikTok

If you connect TikTok:

  • TikTok’s OAuth flow runs in your browser;
  • access / refresh tokens and basic profile fields (such as display name) may be stored locally so the app can upload clips you choose;
  • uploads go to TikTok’s servers under your TikTok account;
  • TikTok’s own privacy policy applies to data they process.

Disconnecting TikTok in the app removes the locally stored TikTok auth file when available.

4. Other third-party services

If you enable cloud transcription or clip-picking providers (for example OpenAI, AssemblyAI, Deepgram, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek), those requests are sent with your own API keys according to each provider’s terms and privacy policy. You control which providers are enabled.

5. YouTube / downloads

If you paste a YouTube (or similar) link, the app may download the video to your PC using local tooling so it can create clips. Follow the platform’s terms for personal use of content you are allowed to process.

6. Analytics and selling data

ahmed clipper does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell your personal information or media.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). Do not use it to process content involving minors in ways that violate platform or legal rules.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The “Last updated” date will change when it does.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: contact the app operator through the email associated with your TikTok for Developers / ahmed clipper project.
